# AGMARKNET: agricultural market information

## What it is

AGMARKNET is a Government of India agricultural marketing information portal. It is a useful starting point for looking at market-related information, including prices and arrivals reported by markets.

Official website: https://agmarknet.gov.in/

## Why we are looking at it

A farmer may know the price offered by a regular buyer but may not know what is being reported in nearby markets. Market information can help provide a wider view before the farmer decides whether to accept an offer or make further enquiries.

## Possible use in Kisan Setu

The prototype could use relevant market information to display reported prices for a selected crop and market. Where the source provides the required fields, the display should retain the market name, date, unit and price category. The platform could then place that information beside a buyer's direct quote.

A comparison should account for differences in crop variety, grade, quantity, distance and payment terms. A market-reported price is not automatically an offer that a farmer can receive.

## Points to check before using the data

- Confirm which fields are available for the selected market and crop.
- Check how often the information is updated and whether any entries are missing or delayed.
- Keep the date, market name, unit and price type with every displayed value.
- Review the portal's current access conditions before automating data collection.
- Do not describe a reported price as a guaranteed sale price.
