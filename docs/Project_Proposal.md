# Kisan Setu: project overview

## The problem we are trying to address

Farmers often make selling decisions with incomplete information. They may know the price offered by a regular buyer but have limited time or means to compare other markets. A higher quoted price elsewhere may not result in higher earnings once transport, handling, storage, quality differences and repayment obligations are considered.

The project focuses on making these factors easier to compare. It is not designed to predict prices with certainty or guarantee a better sale.

## Proposed solution

Kisan Setu is a farmer decision-support platform. It is intended to bring crop details, market information, buyer quotations, cost estimates, storage options and records of input support into one place. The farmer can review the information and decide which option suits their circumstances.

## Main parts of the proposed platform

1. **Farmer and crop profile:** records relevant crop, quantity, location, harvest timing and preferences. Financial details should be optional and collected only when needed.
2. **Market and buyer information:** displays available market prices and direct buyer quotations with their source, date, unit and relevant conditions.
3. **Net-return comparison:** estimates what may remain after known costs and confirmed deductions.
4. **Sell-or-store comparison:** compares immediate sale, partial sale and storage scenarios while showing uncertainty and assumptions.
5. **Input-support record:** helps the farmer keep track of advances, credit and in-kind support.
6. **Communication support:** prepares enquiries for buyers, FPOs, storage providers or lenders, subject to farmer approval.

## Intended users and stakeholders

The primary users are farmers. Other stakeholders may include FPOs, buyers, warehouses, cold-storage operators, agricultural experts and financial institutions. Their details or offers should only be shown after suitable verification and permission.

## What success should mean

The project should be assessed on whether information is accurate, understandable and useful. Possible measures include the time needed to compare options, correctness of cost calculations, farmer understanding, successful contact with verified service providers and feedback on usability.

## Current status and limitations

This document describes the proposed project. Features, partnerships, data feeds and field results should only be described as completed after they have actually been implemented or verified. The first prototype may use clearly labelled sample data and simulated workflows.
