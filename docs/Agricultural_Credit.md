# Agricultural credit and input support

## Why this is part of the project

Some farmers receive money, seeds, fertiliser or other inputs on credit before harvest. Repayment arrangements can affect how much money remains after a crop is sold and may influence when or to whom the farmer sells.

Kisan Setu aims to help farmers keep a clearer record of such obligations and understand how confirmed deductions affect the amount they may receive. The project is not a lender and does not decide whether a person is eligible for a loan.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

The Kisan Credit Card scheme is an established agricultural credit framework. The Reserve Bank of India publishes directions relevant to regulated banks and the scheme. The applicable rules can depend on the type of bank and the current directions in force.

Official RBI reference: https://www.rbi.org.in/SCRIPTS/BS_ViewMasterDirections.aspx?did=343

## Possible use in Kisan Setu

A farmer-controlled support record could capture details such as:

- the amount or value received;
- whether it was cash or an input supplied in kind;
- the date and stated purpose;
- the repayment amount, due date and any disclosed charges;
- the source of the information and whether the farmer has confirmed it.

The platform could use confirmed figures to show an estimated amount remaining after repayment. It should clearly separate a farmer-entered amount from a lender-confirmed balance.

## Important safeguards

- Do not collect account credentials, PINs or OTPs.
- Do not infer loan approval, eligibility or interest rates.
- Do not present an estimate as an official loan statement.
- Show the source and date of any credit terms.
- Encourage the farmer to confirm current terms with the relevant bank or lender.
- Do not share financial information with buyers or other parties without the farmer's explicit approval.
