# Research and References

This folder contains the research and reference material used during the development of Kisan Setu.

Before developing the platform, we wanted to understand the existing agricultural market system, the challenges faced by farmers and the resources already available to support them. The documents in this folder cover different areas that are relevant to our project.

## Research Documents

### 1. AGMARKNET
Information about agricultural market prices and how market data can help farmers compare selling opportunities.

File: `AGMARKNET.md`

### 2. e-NAM
An overview of the National Agriculture Market and its role in connecting agricultural markets through an electronic trading platform.

File: `e-NAM.md`

### 3. Post-Harvest Losses
Research on losses that occur after harvesting, the factors responsible for them and the importance of suitable storage and handling facilities.

File: `Post_Harvest_Losses.md`

### 4. Farmer Producer Organisations
Information about FPOs, their role in supporting farmers and the possibilities of connecting them with the Kisan Setu platform.

File: `FPO_Research.md`

### 5. Agricultural Credit
An overview of agricultural credit facilities, including the Kisan Credit Card scheme and other relevant financial support systems.

File: `Agricultural_Credit.md`

## Purpose of This Research

These documents help us understand the problems Kisan Setu aims to address and identify resources that may be useful during development.

The information is collected from publicly available government websites, reports and other relevant sources. Any proposed use of these resources in Kisan Setu will depend on data availability, access permissions and technical feasibility.

This folder will be updated as we continue our research and development.
