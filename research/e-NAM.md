# e-NAM: National Agriculture Market

## What it is

e-NAM, or the National Agriculture Market, is an electronic trading platform intended to connect participating agricultural markets and support more transparent trade. Its official portal provides information about the programme, participating markets and related services.

Official website: https://www.enam.gov.in/

## Why it matters to this project

Kisan Setu is being designed around the choices a farmer faces before selling a crop. Information about organised market access and electronic trading can help us understand what options may exist beyond a farmer's usual local buyer.

## Possible use in Kisan Setu

The platform could direct farmers to relevant e-NAM information or show verified information that is lawfully available for the markets and commodities they select. It could also help farmers prepare questions about registration, quality requirements, market participation and payment processes.

## What we need to verify

- Whether the farmer's chosen market and commodity are covered.
- What participation, registration and quality-assessment requirements apply.
- Which information is publicly accessible and whether an approved technical interface is available.
- How payments, logistics and dispute processes work for the specific transaction.

The presence of a market on e-NAM does not by itself guarantee that a farmer can sell a particular lot at a particular price. Kisan Setu should not claim that it can execute e-NAM trades unless that capability is actually implemented and authorised.
