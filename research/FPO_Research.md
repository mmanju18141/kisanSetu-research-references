# Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)

## What an FPO does

A Farmer Producer Organisation is a group-based organisation formed by farmers to carry out activities that may be difficult or costly for individual farmers to manage alone. Depending on its capacity and business model, an FPO may help members with input purchases, aggregation, grading, storage, marketing or links to buyers.

The exact services differ from one FPO to another. It is important to check what a particular organisation actually offers rather than assume that every FPO provides the same facilities.

## Why FPOs are relevant to Kisan Setu

Kisan Setu is intended to help farmers understand their options and reach relevant service providers. An FPO may be a useful local contact for information about aggregation, quality requirements, buyer enquiries or shared services.

## Possible use in the platform

With verified information and the organisation's permission, Kisan Setu could:

- list nearby FPO contact details and the crops or services they handle;
- help a farmer prepare an enquiry about aggregation or sale requirements;
- show information supplied by an FPO about quantity, grade, timing and terms;
- help compare an FPO offer with other available options.

Any offer shown should include its date and conditions. The platform should not imply that an FPO has agreed to buy produce unless the offer has been confirmed by that organisation.

## Questions to check with an FPO

- Which villages and crops does it currently serve?
- What quantity and quality standards does it require?
- Does it aggregate, store, grade, transport or market produce?
- What charges, deductions and payment timelines apply?
- Who is the correct contact for confirming current terms?
