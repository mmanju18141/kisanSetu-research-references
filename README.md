# Kisan Setu
### An AI-Powered Platform for Farmer Decision Support

## About the Project

Agriculture involves many decisions beyond growing and harvesting crops. Farmers also need to decide when to sell their produce, which market to approach, whether storage is a suitable option and how different expenses affect their final earnings.

In many cases, making these decisions is difficult because information about market prices, storage facilities and available financial support is scattered across different sources. Farmers may also depend on local traders or have to sell their produce quickly to meet immediate financial requirements.

Kisan Setu is an initiative aimed at addressing these challenges through a single platform. It is being developed to help farmers access relevant information, compare available options and understand the financial outcome of different decisions before selling their produce.

## Key Objectives

The main objective of Kisan Setu is to make agricultural decision-making more informed and accessible. The platform aims to:

- Provide information about agricultural market prices and available buyers.
- Help farmers compare selling prices after considering transportation, handling and other related expenses.
- Assist in evaluating whether selling immediately or storing produce for a later sale is a suitable option.
- Maintain transparent records of advances, loans and other forms of financial support.
- Improve communication between farmers, buyers, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) and storage providers.

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Kisan Setu is planned to use four specialised AI agents. Each agent will handle a particular area of the platform and assist farmers with relevant information and tasks.

### 1. Price Watch Agent

This agent will monitor available market prices and help farmers understand price changes across different markets. It will also indicate the source and freshness of the available information.

### 2. Sell-or-Store Agent

This agent will help farmers compare different selling options. It will consider factors such as current market prices, storage expenses, possible price changes, crop quality and the farmer's immediate financial requirements.

### 3. Storage and Credit Agent

This agent will help identify relevant storage options and provide information about associated costs and available credit facilities. It will also assist farmers in understanding the financial implications of using such services.

### 4. Coordination Agent

This agent will assist with communication by preparing enquiries and messages for buyers, FPOs, warehouses and other service providers. Any communication or external action will require the farmer's approval.

The AI agents are intended to support the decision-making process rather than make decisions independently. Farmers will remain in control of their produce, financial information and transactions.

## Proposed Features

- Farmer registration and crop information management.
- Agricultural market price monitoring.
- Comparison of buyer offers and estimated net earnings.
- Selling and storage decision support.
- Tracking of financial advances and input support.
- AI-assisted communication with relevant stakeholders.
- Information about storage and credit options.

## Development Approach

The project is being developed in stages, with an emphasis on understanding actual agricultural requirements before expanding its functionality.

The initial development process includes studying farmers' challenges, identifying suitable information sources, building the core platform, implementing the decision-support calculations and developing the four AI agents.

The prototype will be tested using different agricultural scenarios. Feedback and further validation will help identify areas that need improvement.

## Future Scope

The long-term aim of Kisan Setu is to develop a practical platform that can address some of the difficulties farmers face while making decisions about their produce.

Future development may include expanding market information sources, improving storage and buyer connections, supporting regional languages and making the platform accessible to farmers with limited internet connectivity.

We intend to improve the project through continued development, testing and feedback from farmers and other stakeholders.
